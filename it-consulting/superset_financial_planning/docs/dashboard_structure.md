# Dashboard Structure

## Executive Cash Flow

**KPI cards**
- Actual Inflow
- Planned Inflow
- Inflow Plan Execution
- Actual Outflow
- Planned Outflow
- Outflow Plan Execution

**Charts**
- Actual vs Planned Cash Flow
- Cash Flow by Business Direction
- Cash Balance Dynamics

## Cash Flow Drivers

**Main visualization**
- Waterfall / bridge chart showing factors behind the change in net cash flow.

## Project Revenue

**Charts**
- Planned Revenue by Project
- Actual Revenue by Project
- Plan vs Actual Variance

## Financial Performance

**Charts**
- Profit by Legal Entity
- Profit by Business Direction
- Revenue / Cost / Margin dynamics
